Where the log goes

Claude
The log is sent to https://api.anthropic.com/v1/messages with your key. It does not pass through a LogFarm server.
This computer
Saved analyses stay as plain text in %APPDATA%\logfarm. A password or token inside the log remains there. A report may show [redacted] instead of the secret. That does not delete the saved log.
License
The license key is checked with Lemon Squeezy. The check sends this computer’s name, as LogFarm- followed by the machine name.
Payment
The card is entered on the Lemon Squeezy checkout page. LogFarm does not store it.
